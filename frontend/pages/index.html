About the Platform

Reserved before arrival. Scanned at the gate. Settled on the spot.

Parking congestion is rarely a capacity problem. It is an information problem. Drivers circle because they cannot see what is free, and the booth slows down because every arrival starts from scratch. This platform closes both gaps with a single shared record of every bay and every booking.

Drivers pick a floor, hold a slot, and arrive with a barcode already issued. Booth staff scan it, take the payment, and print a receipt in seconds. Administrators watch the same live floor map, set the rates, and keep every transaction in one place.
